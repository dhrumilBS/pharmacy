Sound familiar?

Running a medical store is hard enough. Your software shouldn’t add to it.

These are the things pharmacy owners tell us cost them money, time and sleep. Here’s how we handle each one.

We found expired strips behind the new stock — again.

Money already paid to the distributor, now dead stock. And you only find out during a shelf check.

Fix: billing picks the batch that expires first, and you get a 30/60/90-day expiry list in time to sell or return it.

Customers walk out while staff search for the medicine.

Evening rush, five people waiting, and the new staff member can’t find the brand or a substitute.

Fix: search by brand, salt or barcode, see substitutes and stock instantly, and bill from the keyboard.

Every night I sit typing distributor bills, line by line.

Batch, expiry, rate, scheme, free quantity — and you still miss the rate difference you were promised.

Fix: import the distributor’s bill file, and see a warning when a rate is higher than last time.

The software says 5 boxes. The shelf has 2.

Wrong entries, missed returns, or worse. Either way, you stop trusting your stock numbers.

Fix: every sale, return and adjustment is recorded by batch and by user, so you can see exactly where stock went.

GST rates changed. Is my software even updated?

Wrong rates mean wrong invoices, and a month-end mess for you and your CA.

Fix: rates are updated on our side when the law changes, and GST reports are ready for your accountant.

I can’t leave the shop without worrying.

Sales, cash, credit customers, distributor dues — it’s all in the shop, or in a notebook.

Fix: check today’s sales, cash, low stock and pending payments on your phone, from anywhere.

Before you choose

8 questions to ask any pharmacy software company

Whoever you buy from, get clear answers to these. Here are ours.

Ask this Why it matters Our answer
Can a new staff member bill on day one? Staff change often. Training shouldn’t take weeks. Yes — search, pick batch, print. Training is included.
Does billing pick the earliest-expiry batch? This is where most expiry losses start. Yes, automatically, with 30/60/90-day expiry lists.
Will you move my data from my current software? Re-typing thousands of items is not an option. Yes — items, stock, customers and distributors.
What happens when the internet goes down? The counter can’t stop in the evening rush. [Confirm offline billing behaviour before publishing.]
What will I pay in year two? Low first-year prices and costly renewals are common. Renewal price is written in your quote from day one.
Who do I call at 8 pm when billing stops? Support hours should match shop hours. Phone & WhatsApp support during shop hours.
Can I take my data with me if I leave? It’s your business record, not the vendor’s. Yes — export your data any time.
Will it work with my printer and scanner? New hardware is an unplanned cost. Works with common thermal, A4 and barcode devices.
